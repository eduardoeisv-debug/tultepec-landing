## 3. What personal data do we collect?

We only collect personal data when you use the **"Share your memory"** feature. All the data you provide is voluntary:

| Data | Required? | Shown publicly? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name | No. You may leave it blank and share anonymously. | Only if you choose to sign your memory and it is published. |
| Relationship to Tultepec | Depending on the form | Only if your memory is published. |
| Text of your memory | Yes, in order to submit it | Only if your memory is published. |
| Email address | No | **Never.** |

**Sensitive data.** We do not ask for sensitive personal data (such as health, ethnic origin, religious beliefs, political opinions, sexual orientation or financial data). Please do not include it in your memory, nor personal data about other people without their permission. If you include it on your own initiative, we may edit or delete it before any publication.

**Minors.** This site is not directed at people under 18. If you are a minor, please ask your parent or guardian to submit the memory for you.

## 4. What do we use your data for?

**Necessary purposes** (without them we cannot handle your submission):

1. Receive and manually review your memory.
2. Contact you, if you left your email and it is necessary, to clarify or confirm your submission.

**Additional purpose** (requires your consent):

3. Publish your memory, under the name you indicate or anonymously, in this site's archive.

Publication only happens if your memory passes review and you gave your consent when submitting it. You may withdraw that consent at any time (see section 8).

We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for commercial or advertising purposes.

## 8. Your rights (ARCO) and how to exercise them

You have the right to **Access** your data, **Rectify** it, request its **Cancellation** (deletion), and **Oppose** its processing. You may also **withdraw your consent** to the publication of your memory or limit the use of your data, including asking for a memory you already submitted or published to be deleted.

**How to do it:** write to [eduardoeisv@gmail.com](mailto:eduardoeisv@gmail.com) with the subject "ARCO rights" and include:

1. Your name, or the name or pseudonym under which you submitted your memory.
2. The right you wish to exercise and a clear description of your request.
3. Details that help us locate your submission (approximate date and an excerpt of the text).
4. A way for us to reply to you.

Exercising these rights is free of charge. We will respond to your request within the time limits established by law.

If you believe your right to personal data protection has been violated, you may go to the competent authority in Mexico for personal data held by private parties (currently, the Secretaría Anticorrupción y Buen Gobierno).
